Away from home
Missing home but not the people, I talk to them everyday
but losing the spark talking to parents, somehow distant now
the curiosity is fading, its kinda gone 
I miss the home when it was 10 years ago, with my grandma around and I used to go to the roadside shop to buy chatpati for 2 rupess, 5 rupees was a BIG deal

10 days of voluntary Dhul Hijjah fasting, came and went, didn't feel a thing, as if turning into a stone, cried a few times but I still sin, cannot control my desires sometimes
feel like a dummy going about my day, didnt feel my prayers were answers

Really really yearning for a partner
But then I see women today
and then men today
and the thousand cases of injustice and wrong
And then the finger pointing and the blame games which benefits no one
My crush who posts something like "Why you should never marry men" and my heart slumps a little

And then there's the world weighing down
I open instagram and all I see is crime and violence, crime and violence, and hatred, just blind hatred 

The newly elected CM of my state just called for a genocidal like treatment of Muslim minorities in my state

Some IDF soldier proudly posting how they raped a 14 year old Palestinian girl and thought they liked it. Daily news of horrendous crimes against humanity, atrocities that are not even worth naming - pregnant mothers assaulted, family members forced to rape one another, psychological warfare

Then the big techs, billions of dollars of money for unprecedented surveillance states, subtle silencing of minority voices (my Eid stories on Instagram gets roughly 50% as much reach as my other stories)

Then there's my Ummah which has lost its way, descended into laziness, lethargy, complacency and an unplaced trust in God without moving their hands. They say they want the Akhirah and it is okay to be poor in this world. Twisting words to justify their own laziness. My Ummah which should be an exemplar of good conduct, character and the bold paragon to which every non-Muslim compares themselves to and envies - in terms of mental strength, education, phyical strength, health, wellbeing, cleanliness, hygience, mercy, kindness, compassion, and above all justice. All we get is a bunch of deluded, halfly-informed individuals with too much passion but not so much substance engaging in word battles with non-Muslims. Is this how we will set an example for what the Quran teaches? The non-Muslim doesnt read the Quran, they get to know what is in it by observing the Muslim. 


Not much to look forward to and that's killing me slowly


Male loneliness pandemic is real af, I think it's been months since someone appreciated me verbally, forget about a physical affirmation like a pat or a hug

The only thing I look forward to is swimming, really that's all. Its the one time when I can cut myself off and shut down the constant noise and chaos and tugging and pings.
